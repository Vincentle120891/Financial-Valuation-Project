================================================================================
1. MODEL OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Purpose: DCF valuation using two terminal value methods:
  (A) Perpetuity / Gordon Growth — TV = UFCF_terminal / (WACC - g)
  (B) Exit Multiple — TV = Terminal EBITDA × Multiple

Column Mapping (all sheets):
  F = Historical Year 1 (e.g. FY2020)
  G = Historical Year 2 (e.g. FY2021)
  H = Historical Year 3 (e.g. FY2022) ← last actual
  I = Forecast Year 1
  J = Forecast Year 2
  K = Forecast Year 3
  L = Forecast Year 4
  M = Forecast Year 5
  N = Terminal Year

Scenario Switch: Inputs!D7 = Outputs!F6 → 1=Best, 2=Base, 3=Worst

[3A] INCOME STATEMENT — WATERFALL STRUCTURE

  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  INCOME STATEMENT LINE REPAIR WATERFALL (Correct Calculation Order):
  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Gross Profit
    (-) SG&A Expense
    (-) R&D Expense / Other OpEx
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    (=) EBITDA  (This represents pure pre-depreciation cash generation)

    (-) Depreciation & Amortization (Link from your Asset Schedule)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    (=) EBIT / Operating Income (The foundational anchor for your DCF)

    (+) Interest Income (Link from Cash Asset yield)
    (-) Interest Expense (Link from Debt Schedule coupons)
    (+/-) Pure Non-Operating Adjustments (FX gains/losses, legal settlements ONLY)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    (=) Pretax Income (EBT)

    (-) Tax Provision (Calculated using your Adjusted Tax Basis Schedule)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    (=) NET INCOME -> Flows to Cash Flow Statement & Retained Earnings
  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Revenue (row 96):
    Historical: [H] (hardcoded actuals)
    Forecast: [F] =Prior Year × (1 + Revenue Growth)
      where Revenue Growth = CHOOSE-driven from Inputs

  COGS (row 97):
    Historical: [H] (hardcoded actuals, negative)
    Forecast: [F] =Prior Year × (1 + Projected Cost Increase)
      where Projected Cost Increase = CHOOSE-driven from Inputs

  Gross Profit (row 98): [F] =Revenue + COGS (COGS is negative)

  SG&A (row 99):
    Historical: [H] (hardcoded actuals, negative)
    Forecast: [F] =Prior Year × (1 + Projected Cost Increase)

  Other OpEx (row 100) — includes R&D:
    Historical: [H] (hardcoded actuals, negative)
    Forecast: [F] =Prior Year × (1 + Projected Cost Increase)
    NOTE: R&D is bundled into Other OpEx. If separately tracked from XBRL,
          R&D is added to Other OpEx to form the complete cash operating
          expense line. EBITDA = Gross Profit − (SG&A + Other OpEx incl. R&D).

  EBITDA (row 101): [F] =SUM(Gross Profit, SG&A, Other)
    ═══ CRITICAL WATERFALL CHECK ═══
    EBITDA must represent PURE pre-depreciation cash operating generation.
    Formula: EBITDA = Gross Profit − Cash Operating Expenses (SG&A + R&D + Other)
    If your platform computes EBITDA differently (e.g., adds back depreciation
    to EBIT), you have the order inverted and will DOUBLE-COUNT depreciation
    in your FCFF calculation. Verify: EBITDA > EBIT in all periods.

  Depreciation (row 103):
    Historical: [H] (hardcoded actuals, negative — MUST be populated for ALL
                historical periods including 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025)
    Forecast: [L] =linked from Depreciation Schedule (Total Depreciation)
    NOTE: Historical accumulated depreciation on the Balance Sheet (rows 143-144)
          is derived from the running total of this row.

  EBIT (row 104): [F] =EBITDA + Depreciation
    ═══ WATERFALL CHECK ═══
    EBIT = EBITDA − |Depreciation| (since Dep is stored negative).
    This is the foundational anchor for your DCF NOPAT calculation.
    EBIT must equal Operating Income before any financing decisions.

  Interest Expense (row 105):
    Historical: [H] (hardcoded actuals, negative — MUST be populated for ALL
                historical periods. Apple paid ~$3.9B-$4.0B annually.
                Leaving this blank for 2024/2025 creates ghost cash on the
                Balance Sheet and fails identity checks.)
    Forecast: [L] =linked from Debt Schedule Part 2 (Net Interest Expense)
    NOTE: Even though DCF values the firm on an unlevered basis, you still
          need projected Interest Expense to build a complete Income Statement
          for Net Income → Retained Earnings → Balance Sheet reconciliation.

  Interest Income (row 106):
    Historical: [H] (hardcoded actuals, positive — ~$3.0B for Apple)
    Forecast: [L] =linked from Debt Schedule Part 1 (Interest Income)
    CRITICAL: This row tracks ONLY interest earned on cash/marketable securities.
              Do NOT bundle this with Other Income/Expense.

  Other Income/Expense (row 107a):
    Historical: [H] (hardcoded actuals — e.g., -$384M for Apple 2025)
    Forecast: [H] or [L] =non-operating items ONLY
    ═══ DOUBLE-COUNTING RISK ═══
    In Apple's SEC filings, "Other Income/Expense, Net" bundles:
      + Interest Income (~$3.0B)
      - Interest Expense (~$4.0B)
      ± Foreign exchange adjustments
      ± Equity investment gains/losses
      ± Legal settlements
    Since you already have standalone rows for Interest Income (row 106)
    and Interest Expense (row 105), this row MUST ONLY contain pure
    non-interest items: FX gains/losses, equity method adjustments,
    realized investment gains/losses, and one-time legal settlements.
    If your backend API pulled the consolidated SEC line while keeping
    the isolated interest rows active, you are DOUBLE-COUNTING.
    Fix: Other Income/Expense (row 107a) = SEC "Other Income/Expense, Net"
         − Interest Income − Interest Expense

  EBT (row 108): [F] =EBIT + Interest Expense + Interest Income + Other Income/Expense

  Current Tax (row 109):
    Historical: [H] (hardcoded actuals — MUST be populated)
    Forecast: [L] =linked from Income Tax Schedule (Levered), Current Taxes
    Terminal: [F] =EBT × Tax Rate (no tax losses remaining)

  Deferred Tax (row 110):
    Historical: [H] (hardcoded actuals)
    Forecast: [L] =linked from Income Tax Schedule (Levered), Deferred Taxes
    Terminal: [H] 0
    NOTE: Deferred Tax = timing difference between accounting and tax treatment.
          This flows to the Cash Flow Statement as a non-cash add-back and to
          the Balance Sheet as Deferred Tax Liabilities (net DTA/DTL position).

  Total Tax (row 111): [F] =Current Tax + Deferred Tax

  Net Income (row 112): [F] =EBT + Total Tax
    → Flows to: Cash Flow Statement (row 115), Equity Schedule (Retained Earnings)

[3B] CASH FLOW STATEMENT — SIGN CONVENTION ENFORCED

  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  CASH FLOW STATEMENT INTEGRITY WATERFALL
  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Operating Cash Flow (CFO Baseline)
    (-) Strip out Non-Operating Interest Income (if bundled in CFO)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    (=) TRUE OPERATING CASH FLOW

  Investing Cash Flow (CFI Module)
    (-) Capex (Must be explicitly NEGATIVE, NO duplicate positive row)
    (+) Inflows from Marketable Securities Liquidations (if applicable)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    (=) SUBTOTAL CASH FLOW FROM INVESTING (CFI)

  Financing Cash Flow (CFF Module)
    (+) Debt Issuance (Positive cash influx)
    (-) Debt Repayments (Must be NEGATIVE)
    (-) Dividends Paid (Must be NEGATIVE)
    (-) Share Buybacks / RSU Tax Settlements (Must be NEGATIVE)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    (=) SUBTOTAL CASH FLOW FROM FINANCING (CFF)

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FINAL RECONCILIATION CELL:
  Net Change in Cash = True CFO + CFI + CFF
  → Links directly to Balance Sheet Cash (row 138)
  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  ── SIGN CONVENTION RULES (CRITICAL) ──
  ════════════════════════════════════════
  Every row in the Cash Flow Statement MUST follow these sign rules:
    • Cash INFLOWS = Positive values
    • Cash OUTFLOWS = Negative values
  Specifically:
    • CapEx: MUST be NEGATIVE (cash leaving the firm)
    • Dividends: MUST be NEGATIVE (cash paid to shareholders)
    • Debt Repayments: MUST be NEGATIVE (cash paid to creditors)
    • Debt Issuance: MUST be POSITIVE (cash received from creditors)
    • Share Buybacks: MUST be NEGATIVE (cash paid to repurchase shares)
    • Depreciation Add-back: POSITIVE (non-cash expense added back)
    • Deferred Tax Add-back: Sign matches tax schedule output
  ════════════════════════════════════════

  ── DUPLICATE ROW PREVENTION ──
  ════════════════════════════════════════
  There must be EXACTLY ONE CapEx row in the Cash Flow Statement.
  If your data ingestion API pulls the same metric from two different
  database tags (e.g., "capex" = -$12.7B AND "CapEx" = +$12.7B),
  they will cancel each other out: -$12.7B + $12.7B = $0.
  This makes the model think the company spends $0 on capital assets,
  completely distorting Net PP&E projections and useful life calculations.
  FIX: Keep ONLY the NEGATIVE CapEx row. Drop any positive duplicate.
  ════════════════════════════════════════

  ── CFO SECTION ──

  Net Income (row 115): [L] =linked from Income Statement (row 112)
  Deferred Taxes (row 116): [L] =linked from Income Statement (row 110)
    NOTE: Deferred Tax flows through CFO as a non-cash add-back.
          If DTA increased → cash outflow (negative adjustment).
          If DTL increased → cash inflow (positive adjustment).
  Depreciation (row 117): [L] =ABS(linked from Income Statement depreciation) [POSITIVE]
    NOTE: Depreciation is stored NEGATIVE on the Income Statement.
          On the CFS it MUST be POSITIVE (added back as non-cash expense).

  Cash From AR (row 118):
    Historical: [H] — MUST be populated for all historical periods
    Forecast: [F] =Prior AR Balance − Current AR Balance (from Working Capital)
    Logic: If AR decreases → customers paid → cash INFLOW (positive)

  Cash From Inventory (row 119):
    Historical: [H]
    Forecast: [F] =Prior Inventory − Current Inventory
    Logic: If Inventory decreases → sold off stock → cash INFLOW (positive)

  Cash From AP (row 120):
    Historical: [H]
    Forecast: [F] =Current AP − Prior AP
    Logic: If AP increases → delayed payments → cash INFLOW (positive)

  Subtotal CFO (row 121): [F] =SUM(Net Income through Cash From AP)

  ── CFI SECTION ──

  Capital Expenditure (row 123):
    Historical: [H] — MUST be NEGATIVE for all historical periods
    Forecast: [L] =linked from Depreciation Schedule (CapEx, NEGATIVE)
    ═══ SINGLE ROW ONLY ═══
    There must NOT be a second "CapEx" or "capital_expenditure" row.
    If your data has two rows (one negative, one positive), REMOVE the
    positive duplicate. The model's CFI subtotal will be WRONG if both
    rows are summed: they cancel to zero.

  Subtotal CFI (row 124): [F] =CapEx (single negative value)

  ── CFF SECTION ──

  Change in LT Debt (row 126):
    Historical: [H]
    Forecast: [H] =hardcoded from Inputs row 23
    Sign: POSITIVE = new debt issued, NEGATIVE = debt repaid

  Change in Revolving Credit (row 127):
    Historical: [H] (zeros)
    Forecast: [L] =linked from Debt Schedule Part 2 (Revolving Credit change)
    Sign: POSITIVE = drawn down, NEGATIVE = repaid

  Change in Common Equity (row 128):
    Historical: [H]
    Forecast: [H] =hardcoded from Inputs row 24
    Sign: POSITIVE = new equity issued, NEGATIVE = buybacks

  Dividends (row 129):
    Historical: [H]
    Forecast: [L] =linked from Equity Schedule (Dividend, NEGATIVE)
    Sign: MUST be NEGATIVE (cash paid out to shareholders)

  Subtotal CFF (row 130): [F] =SUM(Change in LT Debt through Dividends)

  ── CASH BALANCE ──

  Beginning Cash (row 132):
    Historical: [H]
    Forecast: [F] =Prior year ending balance

  Change (row 133): [F] =CFO + CFI + CFF
    ═══ RECONCILIATION CHECK ═══
    This value MUST equal the change in cash on the Balance Sheet:
    Change (row 133) = Balance Sheet Cash(ending) − Balance Sheet Cash(beginning)

  Ending Cash (row 134): [F] =Beginning + Change
    → Links to: Balance Sheet Cash (row 138)

      ═══ CRITICAL MISSING ITEM — DTA vs DTL SEPARATION ═══
      Under US GAAP, companies net Deferred Tax Assets (DTAs) and
      Deferred Tax Liabilities (DTLs). Your data table may list:
        Deferred Tax Assets as positive (e.g., $27.5B in 2025)
        Deferred Tax as negative (e.g., -$2.1B)

        If Apple has massive non-cash tax timing differences (DTAs/DTLs),
        your model must add back or subtract those deferred adjustments
        on the Cash Flow Statement to compute pure, un-distorted FCF.
      Formula: Deferred Tax Liabilities = DTL − DTA (net position)
        If net positive → liability (cash not yet paid)
        If net negative → asset (cash prepaid / refund receivable)

8. KEY ASSUMPTIONS
================================================================================

  ── REVENUE & EXPENSES ──
  1. Revenue = Prior × (1 + Revenue Growth) — CHOOSE-driven
  2. COGS/OpEx grow at Cost Projection Increase (CHOOSE-driven), NOT revenue growth
  3. R&D is bundled into Other OpEx for EBITDA calculation
  4. EBITDA = Gross Profit − (SG&A + Other OpEx incl. R&D) — pure pre-depreciation cash generation
  5. EBIT = EBITDA − Depreciation — foundational anchor for DCF NOPAT

  ── WORKING CAPITAL ──
  6. WC days constant through terminal year
  7. Current Liabilities identity: CL = Payables + Accrued Expenses + Other CL
     + Current Debt & Capital Lease Oblig + Current Deferred Liabilities
     — critical for accurate ΔNWC calculations
  8. Current Accrued Expenses tracked SEPARATELY from Other Current Liabilities
     to allow granular working capital analysis

  ── DEPRECIATION & ASSETS ──
  8. Depreciation: SL for existing (16yr) and new (20yr) assets
  9. Tax depreciation: declining balance 15% with 50% half-year convention
  10. Historical Accumulated Depreciation MUST be populated for ALL periods
      (2022-2025) to establish correct opening net asset baseline
  11. PP&E Net = PP&E Gross − |Accumulated Depreciation| on Balance Sheet

  ── TAX ──
  12. Tax losses carry forward indefinitely; no carryback; separate levered/unlevered pools
  13. Deferred Tax Liabilities = net of DTA − DTL position (non-cash timing differences)
  14. Deferred taxes add back on CFO to compute pure un-distorted Free Cash Flow

  ── INTEREST & DEBT ──
  15. Interest is VARIABLE (from debt schedules): LT=6%, Revolving=5%, Cash=1%
  16. Interest Expense MUST be populated for ALL historical periods (2022-2025)
      to prevent ghost cash on the Balance Sheet
  17. Other Income/Expense contains ONLY non-interest items (FX gains/losses,
      equity investment adjustments, legal settlements) — no double-counting
      with Interest Income or Interest Expense rows

  ── FINANCING ──
  18. Dividends = Net Income × Payout Ratio
  19. Revolving Credit dynamically calculated from available cash

  ── BALANCE SHEET ──
  20. BALANCE SHEET IDENTITIES (ALL must hold in ALL periods):
      Assets = Liabilities + Equity
        Current Assets = Cash + ST Investments + Receivables + Inventory + Other CA
        Non-Current Assets = Net PPE + Investments & Advances + Other NCA + Deferred Assets
        Total Assets = Current Assets + Non-Current Assets
        Current Liabilities = Payables + Accrued Expenses + Other CL + Curr Debt + Deferred
        Non-Current Liabilities = LT Debt & Cap Lease + Trade Payables NC + Other NC Liab
        Total Liabilities = Current Liabilities + Non-Current Liabilities
        Total Equity = Common Stock + Retained Earnings + Other Equity Adjustments
        Total L&E = Total Liabilities + Total Equity
  21. Investments And Advances (long-term bond portfolio) MUST be included in
      Non-Current Assets — critical for EV-to-Equity bridge:
      Equity Value = EV − Debt + Cash + ALL Marketable Securities
  22. Other Current Assets, Other Non Current Assets, Other NC Liabilities,
      and Other Equity Adjustments MUST be populated from XBRL data
  23. Balance sheet balances in all years (Check = 0) — three-statement identity

  ── CASH FLOW STATEMENT ──
  24. EXACTLY ONE CapEx row — must be NEGATIVE (no duplicate positive row)
  25. All sign conventions enforced: outflows negative, inflows positive
  26. Net Change in Cash = CFO + CFI + CFF → links to Balance Sheet Cash

  ── VALUATION ──
  27. Two UFCF methods (EBITDA & NI) must reconcile exactly
  28. Partial-period adjustment for stub period
